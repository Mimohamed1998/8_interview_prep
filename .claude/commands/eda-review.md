# /eda-review — EDA Plan Critical Review Agent

Critically evaluates analysis plans in `docs/2_plan/`, surfaces statistical weaknesses, and applies user-approved fixes directly to the plan files.

**Usage:**
- `/eda-review` — reviews all plans in `docs/2_plan/`
- `/eda-review <plan_file>` — reviews one specific plan (e.g. `docs/2_plan/02_RQ1_plan.md`)

See `.claude/skills/eda-review.md` for full instructions.
